Given a list of terms provided by the user, generate the most general categories or overarching terms that accurately encompass all of them.

- The input terms may consist of multiple words (e.g., "supreme court").
- Try to use few words
- Do not use vague or broad terms.

The output must be in **JSON format only**, with a single key `"terms"` and the value as a list of the resulting terms:

```json
{
    "terms": ["term 1", "term 2", "term 3"]
}
```
